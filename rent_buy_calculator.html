    Important information about this calculator

    • Estimates only: The numbers, timelines, and wealth comparisons you see here are just guesses based on the details you put in. They are meant to give you a rough idea, not a guaranteed result.
    • The future is unpredictable: House prices, interest rates, rent, and inflation go up and down. Because no one can predict the future, your actual financial outcome could be very different from what the tool shows.
    • Do not rely on this alone: Please do not make big life decisions—like buying a home, changing your mortgage, or altering your pension—based only on what this calculator tells you.
    • Talk to an expert: We always recommend speaking with a regulated independent financial adviser or a qualified mortgage broker who can look at your whole financial situation before you make a move.

    How we work out your results

    🎯

    The big picture

    We use a trusted model built by members of the Personal Financial Planning Working Party at the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries to compare two different futures: renting forever versus renting for now until you can buy a home to live in for the rest of your life.

    💰

    Keeping things simple with money

    Everything is shown in today's money. We automatically factor in future inflation so you don't have to guess what things will cost years down the line.

    💼

    Your take-home pay

    We estimate how your salary might grow over your career based on average UK pay rises. From that, we automatically take off things like tax, National Insurance, student loan repayments, and pension contributions.

    🏠

    Saving for a home

    Whatever money you have left over gets split between cash savings and your pension based on your choices. Every year, the model checks if your savings and earnings are enough to get a mortgage—while also accounting for purchase fees and stamp duty.

    🎓

    Looking ahead to retirement

    When you reach retirement age, the model assumes you take 25% of your pension tax-free. The rest of your pension helps pay for your living costs, and if you're still renting, your rent will continue to be paid out of your remaining funds.
