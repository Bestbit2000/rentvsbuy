Alex, 21

The "Unwilling Boomerang"

"I love my parents, but I need to be able to order a pizza at midnight without answering twenty questions. My living situation is actively killing my social life."

The Current Situation

Alex moved back into his childhood bedroom after graduation. His parents charge him a small amount of board, but he feels like a teenager with an adult salary. He wants to buy a £200,000 one-bedroom flat in St Albans.

Income & Debt

£32,000 salary

£53,000 student loan

Outgoings

£800 /mo rent

£1,250 /mo lifestyle

Savings

£500 in bank

£0 in pension

The Goal

£200,000

1-bed flat

38 Purchase Age

The Base Outcome

If Alex changes nothing, targeting a house purchase is still his best financial strategy. However, he will be 38 years old before he can afford to buy his flat—four years later than the current UK average for first-time buyers.

Buying > Renting

Even buying at 38, he generates £396,000 more in lifetime wealth compared to renting for life, largely due to escaping rent costs in retirement.

How can Alex speed up the process?

Age 38 feels a long way off for a 21-year-old. Our model tested different life choices and interventions to see how Alex could buy his home sooner.

The "Bank of Mum & Dad"

If Alex's parents are in a position to help, transferring wealth early in life has a massive multiplier effect.

Scenario A: Free Rent

If his parents stop charging the £800/mo rent.

New Purchase Age: 27

He buys 11 years earlier.

Scenario B: A £10,000 Gift

A one-off lump sum contribution to his deposit.

New Purchase Age: 36
Added Lifetime Wealth: +£67,000

A £10k gift grows to create nearly 7x that amount in lifetime wealth.

Changing Lifestyle Habits

Alex currently spends £1,250 a month on clothes, nights out, and trips. What happens if he embraces a frugal lifestyle?

Scenario C: Cutting Expenses

Reducing discretionary spending from £1,250 down to £500 a month.

New Purchase Age: 27
Total Lifetime Wealth: £3.3M

"How you spend the money you have makes a significant impact on both overall wealth accumulation over your lifetime and when you can buy a house."

By cutting back, he buys 11 years earlier and dramatically increases his final net worth from the base £1.35M.
